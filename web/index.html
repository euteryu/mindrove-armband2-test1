Impedance readout

Raw values as reported by the band. Which of the 10 values is the reference or bias electrode is not documented, so compare them with each other: one value far above the rest suggests that pod has poor skin contact.

Is the armband working properly?

A guided test of about 45 seconds. Follow the prompts; the program measures each channel and judges it for you.

  1. Wear the band snug on the thickest part of your forearm, on slightly damp skin, and wait a minute.
  2. Sit with your arm supported and relaxed.
  3. You will be asked to relax, make a fist, bend the wrist both ways, then twist the forearm.

Wear session

One fixed recording each time you put the band on (about 5 minutes): two reference holds, then 😌 rest, ✊ fist, 🖐 open, ↙ wrist in and 🤟 Spider-Man in three arm positions, then a minute of everyday movement. It trains a model for this wear straight away and saves the session for the cross-wear models (docs/ROADMAP.md).

Teach it your gestures

You hold each gesture for a few seconds, several rounds. Retrain whenever you take the band off and put it back on.
